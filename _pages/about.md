I am a final-year Ph.D. researcher at **The University of Manchester**, in the *<u>Department of Computer Science</u>* (*<u>Machine Learning and Robotics</u>* group). My research lies at the intersection of second-order optimization and reinforcement learning, with a focus on developing Kalman filtering methods for efficient and scalable natural policy gradient estimation. My work spans theoretical convergence analysis and practical policy optimization, with applications to robotic and embodied systems. I am particularly interested in decision-making and control using large-scale vision-language and foundation models.
