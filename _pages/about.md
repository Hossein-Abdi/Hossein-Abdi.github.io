Ph.D. student in Computer Science (**Machine Learning and Robotics**) at **The University of Manchester**, with research interests in *<u>second-order optimization</u>*, <u>*policy gradient reinforcement learning*</u>, <u>*control theory*</u>, and <u>*robotics*</u>. I hold M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees in Mechanical Engineering from Sharif University of Technology, with a focus on robotics and control theory. My current Ph.D. research focuses on Kalman-based methods for recursively estimating the natural policy gradient, with the goal of enabling efficient and scalable second-order optimization in reinforcement learning.
