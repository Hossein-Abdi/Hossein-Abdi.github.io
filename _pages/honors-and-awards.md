**Scholarship Awarded**, 2023  
Recipient of the highly prestigious Dean's Doctoral Scholarship Award from The University of Manchester.  

**Gold Medal in Olympiad**, 2017  
Awarded the Gold Medal (First Rank) in the National Olympiad of Mechanical Engineering, a competition held among the top undergraduate students in the country.  

**Merit-Based Admission Offer**, 2017  
Awarded a direct admission offer, exempt from the entrance exam, to the M.Sc. program in Mechanical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology in recognition of outstanding academic excellence.  

**Top Rank in M.Sc. Entrance Exam**, 2017  
Achieved 10th and 4th ranks in the nationwide entrance examinations for the M.Sc. Programs in Mechanical Engineering and Aerospace Engineering, respectively.
